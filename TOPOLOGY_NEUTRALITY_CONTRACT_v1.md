# TOPOLOGY NEUTRALITY CONTRACT v1

## 1. Constitutional Authority
This contract permanently defines ChronoSentiment as an **Execution Climatology Laboratory** (Path A). It formally prohibits the framework from evolving into a **Topology-Adaptive Trading Framework** (Path B). The laboratory exists to measure and map topological physics, NOT to exploit them. 

## 2. The Core Invariant
**Alpha must remain permanently topology-blind.** 
Execution constraints may alter realized economic exposure, but cognition generation must remain entirely decoupled from environmental physics. 

## 3. Forbidden Mechanics
The following mechanics constitute "Climatology Exploitation Drift" and are strictly prohibited across the entire architecture:
1. **Topology-Conditioned Strategy Selection**: Automatically selecting or weighting strategies based on real-time or historical Topology Severity Index (TSI).
2. **Topology-Aware Capital Allocation**: Dynamically increasing or decreasing position sizing or portfolio exposure based on perceived "favorable" observability regimes.
3. **Regime-Based Activation/Deactivation**: Turning strategies on or off based on the detection of fragmentation clusters (e.g., wavefronts or bimodal delays).
4. **Topology-Informed Signal Generation**: Allowing cognition windows or signal thresholds to adapt to measured lag dispersion or memory coherence decay.

## 4. Enforcement of Non-Realization
The observation that "Execution constraints altered realized economic exposure" (e.g., negative Economic Divergence where blocked trades saved capital) must be treated purely as an ecological phenomenon. It must NEVER be reverse-engineered into an execution filter or admissibility rule.

Any code change that attempts to use observability metrics as predictive or protective trading variables violates the core causal isolation of this laboratory and must be rejected.

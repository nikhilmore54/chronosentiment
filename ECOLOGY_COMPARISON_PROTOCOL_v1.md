# ECOLOGY COMPARISON PROTOCOL v1

## 1. Core Mandate
This document governs the scientific comparison of multiple Alpha strategies within the Execution Ecology framework. As ChronoSentiment scales into multi-strategy experimentation, it is prohibited to collapse ecological survivability into single-scalar rankings. Strategies are not "better" or "worse"; they belong to different *ecological species* with distinct environmental dependencies.

## 2. Permitted Comparison Metrics
Cross-strategy evaluations must exclusively compare multi-dimensional fingerprints:
- **Synchronization Dependency**: A strategy's reliance on `SYNCHRONIZED` regimes.
- **Fragmentation Tolerance**: A strategy's ability to maintain execution continuity during bimodal or wave-based lag structures.
- **Topology Coupling Strength**: The degree to which strategy execution frequency correlates with the Topology Severity Index (TSI).

## 3. Prohibited Ranking Semantics (Ecological Mythology)
- **NO "Best" Strategy**: Strategies may not be ranked into a singular hierarchy based on ecology. A strategy that survives high fragmentation is merely adapted for dispersion, not objectively superior to one that requires synchronization.
- **NO Scoring Consolidation**: You may not multiply or sum the five ecology properties into a master "Survivability Score." Dimensionality must be preserved.

## 4. Ecological Dimensionality Rules
When publishing research or comparing fingerprints, the full vector must be preserved. A strategy's resilience under `topo_collapse_300` must be presented alongside its behavior under `topo_uniform_60`. Collapsing the space hides causal dependencies.

## 5. Topology Normalization & Invariants
- Strategies can only be compared if their ecology fingerprints were generated against the **exact same deterministic topology injection** applied to the **exact same chronological baseline ledger**.
- If two strategies produce differing `intent_sequence_stable` validations, the comparison is invalid. Both must prove causal isolation from the environment before their executions can be compared.

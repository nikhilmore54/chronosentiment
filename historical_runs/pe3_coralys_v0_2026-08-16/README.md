## Replay pipeline

For each historical timestamp T in the eligible universe:

1. Generate C3-002 direction using bars ≤ T (frozen implementation).
2. Seal direction.
3. Find next eligible session E.
4. Obtain historical session-open price at E.
5. Calculate ATR(14) using bars ≤ T.
6. Apply frozen coralys-exec-v0 artifact.
7. Generate `target_pct`, `target_price`, `risk_pct`, `risk_boundary`.
8. Replay subsequent 20 sessions.
9. Determine first valid exit: `TARGET | RISK | HORIZON | TARGET_GAP_THROUGH | RISK_GAP_THROUGH | AMBIGUOUS`.
10. Write record to P.E.3 historical ledger.
11. Write feedback record: `feedback_available_at`, `execution_feature_hash`, `record_hash`, `learning_scope = ExecutionOnly`.

**Eligibility rule:** Positions where ATR(14) > 0 at decision time T.
Positions where ATR = 0 or unavailable are excluded (`CoralysExecutionResult::Invalid`).
They are **not** replaced with a P.E.2 +5% fallback.

## Immutability

Once `replay_completed_at` is set in `metadata.json`, this archive becomes **IMMUTABLE**.

The coralys artifact hash must not change:
`3876ffa232f75068636aa058c6775671ac2f935ad2751c1253edd49e0770883f`

Historical replay outcomes populate the evidence ledger but are **not** treated as newly available learning feedback for coralys-v1 merely because they were replayed. The distinction between *retrospective characterization* and *information available at historical training time* must be preserved.

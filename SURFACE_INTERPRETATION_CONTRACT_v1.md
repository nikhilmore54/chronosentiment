# SURFACE INTERPRETATION CONTRACT v1

## 1. Constitutional Authority
This contract governs the visual and analytical interpretation of the Ecological Survivability Surfaces. As the laboratory begins to project the intersection of Topology, State, Economics, and Chronology, the risk of "Interpretation Drift" becomes the primary threat to causal isolation. 

## 2. Forbidden Interpretations
The following cognitive leaps and analytical practices are permanently banned:
1. **Desirability Inference**: Stating or implying that a specific topology is "better" or "optimal" for a strategy.
2. **Topology Preference Learning**: Attempting to reverse-engineer environmental blockades into execution filters or admissibility criteria.
3. **Surface Ranking**: Attempting to sort or rank multiple strategies based on the geometric properties of their survivability surfaces.
4. **Adaptive Climatology Fitting**: Tuning alpha parameters to artificially flatten a surface against historical topological stress.

## 3. Mandatory Interpretation Framing
Any human or programmatic analysis of a Survivability Surface must adhere to the following framework:
- It is a **Descriptive Topological Mapping**.
- It visualizes the **Execution-Qualified Deformation** of a fixed cognition under variable stress.
- Asymmetry in the surface (e.g., negative Economic Divergence under heavy fragmentation) is a **physical observation of non-realization**, not an exploitable edge.

Any UI or projection layer must present the 4 planes independently and without overarching summary scores.

# GENERALIZATION BOUNDARY CONTRACT v1

## 1. Constitutional Authority
This contract governs the epistemic boundaries of the ChronoSentiment laboratory. As the system scales to continuous topologies and observes structural memory behavior, the risk of "Theory Drift"—specifically Premature Universality Inference—becomes a critical threat. This contract explicitly limits conclusions to the observed environment and prohibits universal architectural claims that exceed the mathematical evidence.

## 2. Prohibition of Absolute Ontology
The laboratory observes finite phenomena within bounded horizons. Therefore, the use of absolute or infinite terminology is strictly prohibited. 

**Mandatory Semantic Reframing:**
- **Forbidden:** "Permanent" or "Infinite divergence"
- **Mandatory:** "Non-convergent within observed horizon"

- **Forbidden:** "Recoverable" or "Fully Healed"
- **Mandatory:** "Convergent within observed horizon"

## 3. Limits of Inference
All discoveries regarding memory convergence geometry, persistence thresholds, or continuity deformation must be explicitly localized to the exact intersection of variables tested.

**Forbidden Inference Patterns:**
1. **Substrate Generalization**: Claiming that an observation on `batch_10001` (Crypto/Continuous) universally applies to `batch_10000` (Equities/Session-Bound) without empirical verification.
2. **Topology Generalization**: Claiming that a convergence threshold observed under `topo_wavefront` represents the fundamental nature of the strategy across all forms of stress (e.g., `topo_uniform_delay`).
3. **Causal Overreach**: Stating that a system is "structurally incompatible" with continuous execution based on a single topology's resonance effect.

## 4. The Principle of Contingent Observation
Any newly identified behavioral threshold (e.g., the window size boundary between convergence and non-convergence) must initially be treated as a **Contingent Property of the specific Topology-Memory interaction**, not a universal architectural law. The existence of a threshold merely warrants a cross-topology convergence sweep to determine whether the threshold is memory-intrinsic or resonance-induced.

Any interpretation that violates these bounds must be rejected as unscientific optimization mythology.

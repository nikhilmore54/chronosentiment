# PNL ATTRIBUTION CONTRACT v1

## 1. Core Mandate
This contract formally separates the measurement of alpha quality (intent generation) from the measurement of execution outcomes (economic realization) under the influence of topological physics. PnL is exclusively an observational metric of ecological survivability, not an objective function for optimization.

## 2. Forbidden Architecture
The execution pipeline must **NEVER** use observability metrics to directly scale, filter, or manipulate trade outcomes dynamically. For example, scaling position size based on `SYNCHRONIZED` regime state or altering leverage based on `Memory Coherence Index` is strictly forbidden. This would collapse topology into market belief, violating causal separation.

## 3. The PnL Identity Equation
Economic outcome in the Execution Laboratory is defined as the mechanical intersection of the following, and nothing else:
`Strategy Intent` ∩ `Execution Physics` ∩ `Topology` ∩ `Chronology` = `Execution-Qualified PnL`

## 4. Execution Rules
1. **Fixed Size**: All positions must execute with a static size of 1.
2. **Fixed Duration/Condition**: Entry and exit must be strictly defined by the canonical or repaired intent generation rules.
3. **No Hidden Costs**: Slippage models, latency arbitrage mechanisms, or fee modeling are strictly excluded from the baseline Execution-Qualified PnL until formalized in a separate contract.
4. **Attribution Sovereignty**: Every dollar gained or lost must be directly traceable to one of four origins:
    - Pure cognitive signal success/failure (Canonical PnL)
    - Execution Blockade (Topology Physics)
    - Repair-Induced Divergence
    - Persistence Scar (Hysteresis decay)

## 5. Permitted Economic Ecology Metrics
- `PnL Under Topology`: Raw economic result of the strategy when constrained by execution physics.
- `PnL Variance`: Difference between Canonical PnL and Execution-Qualified PnL.
- `Blocked Trades vs Avoided Losses`: Counting instances where an admissibility block saved the strategy from a losing canonical trade, versus costing it a winning trade.
- `Economic Recovery Half-Life`: The time required for PnL generation to resynchronize with the canonical equity curve post-fragmentation.

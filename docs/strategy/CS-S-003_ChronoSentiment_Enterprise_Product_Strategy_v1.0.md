## The Problem ChronoSentiment Enterprise Solves

Investment management firms make consequential decisions at high frequency. A portfolio manager makes 3–8 consequential investment decisions per week. Each decision involves research, hypothesis formation, committee review, execution, and outcome — a structured lifecycle that is almost never documented as a lifecycle.

The result is a structural knowledge problem that compounds over time:

**Decision provenance is lost.** When a position is reviewed six months later, the original reasoning is gone. The analyst who built the thesis has moved on, or the notes are in an inbox, or the model has been updated without a record of what changed and why.

**Institutional knowledge evaporates.** When a senior analyst leaves, years of accumulated investment knowledge leave with them. The firm cannot search its own history. It re-researches companies it has covered before.

**AI decisions are undocumented.** Investment teams are already using AI tools in their research and decision process. The AI's contribution to the decision is invisible — there is no record of what the AI said, what the analyst accepted, what they rejected, and why.

**Regulatory exposure is growing.** The EU AI Act, FCA guidance, and SEC commentary are creating explicit requirements for AI documentation in investment decisions. Firms that cannot demonstrate a structured, auditable decision process face increasing regulatory risk.

**The learning loop is broken.** Investment decisions are made, executed, and reviewed — but the lessons are rarely captured in a form that informs future decisions. The firm does not systematically learn from its own history.

ChronoSentiment Enterprise solves this by providing a structured environment for investment decision intelligence — from thesis formation through committee review to outcome recording and organisational learning.

## What ChronoSentiment Enterprise Is

ChronoSentiment Enterprise is a **Financial Decision Intelligence Platform**. It provides investment teams with:

- **Decision Workspace** — a structured environment for each investment decision, from thesis formation through execution and outcome
- **Decision Timeline** — a chronological record of how every investment thesis evolved
- **Decision Memory** — a searchable archive of every investment decision, with full provenance
- **Decision Provenance** — a complete audit trail of who believed what, when, and why
- **Decision Governance** — structured committee review workflows with documented outcomes
- **Organisational Learning** — a knowledge graph that accumulates investment insights across all decisions

The customer does not need to know that this is built on the Coralys Knowledge Evolution Platform. The customer sees better investment decisions, better governance, and institutional memory that compounds over time.

## Why Investment Management First

Investment management has a unique combination of characteristics that makes the decision documentation problem both more painful and more urgent than in other domains:

**High decision frequency.** A portfolio manager makes 3–8 consequential investment decisions per week. The volume of decisions that need to be documented is an order of magnitude higher than in most other knowledge-intensive professions.

**High stakes per decision.** A single investment decision can represent tens or hundreds of millions of pounds. The cost of a poorly documented decision — in LP relations, regulatory exposure, or institutional learning — is correspondingly high.

**Explicit accountability.** Investment managers are explicitly accountable for their decisions to LPs, regulators, and boards. The accountability structure creates demand for documentation that does not exist in the same form in other professions.

**AI adoption is already happening.** Investment teams are already using AI tools in their research and decision process. The documentation gap is already open.

**Regulatory pressure is immediate.** The EU AI Act, FCA guidance, and SEC commentary are creating explicit requirements for AI documentation in investment decisions now.

## Target Market

**Primary segment:** Independent asset managers (AUM £500M–£5B) with structured investment processes and 5–30 investment professionals. These firms are large enough to have coordination and documentation problems but small enough that bespoke enterprise software is out of reach.

**Secondary segment:** Hedge funds (5–30 investment professionals) with high-velocity research workflows where speed of synthesis and decision quality are direct competitive advantages.

**Tertiary segment:** Family offices with structured investment processes and accountability to family principals.

**Out of scope (v1.0):** Retail investors, solo practitioners, and firms with fewer than 5 investment professionals.

## Defensibility

The most important strategic question is: why won't a larger, better-resourced competitor simply build this?

The answer is not that it is technically difficult. The defensibility comes from five reinforcing barriers that compound over time.

**Barrier 1 — The Decision Archive.** The longer a firm uses ChronoSentiment Enterprise, the more valuable the product becomes. After one year, the firm has a searchable archive of every investment decision, with full provenance, outcome data, and lessons captured. After three years, the archive is a strategic asset that cannot be reconstructed from scratch. A new entrant cannot replicate a three-year decision archive.

**Barrier 2 — Workflow Integration.** ChronoSentiment Enterprise integrates into the investment team's daily workflow — thesis formation, committee review, portfolio monitoring. Once integrated, the switching cost is not the software cost; it is the cost of losing the decision archive and rebuilding the workflow.

**Barrier 3 — Organisational Learning.** The Institutional Decision Knowledge Graph accumulates across all decisions. The longer the firm uses the product, the more the knowledge graph reflects the firm's specific investment philosophy, sector expertise, and decision patterns. This is not generic knowledge — it is the firm's own institutional intelligence.

**Barrier 4 — Regulatory Compliance Infrastructure.** As regulatory requirements for AI documentation in investment decisions increase, ChronoSentiment Enterprise becomes compliance infrastructure. Compliance infrastructure is not switched out lightly.

**Barrier 5 — Reference Network Effects.** CIOs talk to each other. A single credible reference at a respected mid-market asset manager is worth more than ten references in a less networked industry. Early reference customers create a network effect that accelerates adoption.

## Why ChronoSentiment Enterprise Is Different

Most investment management software is either data infrastructure (Bloomberg, FactSet) or workflow tools (portfolio management systems, order management systems). Neither category addresses the decision documentation problem.

| Competitor | What they do | Why ChronoSentiment Enterprise is different |
|------------|-------------|---------------------------------------------|
| Bloomberg Terminal | Market data and news | No decision documentation; no thesis management; no organisational learning |
| FactSet | Financial data and analytics | No decision documentation; no AI documentation; no knowledge graph |
| Visible Alpha | Consensus data | Narrow scope; no decision lifecycle; no institutional memory |
| Sentieo / Tegus | Document search and research | No decision lifecycle; no committee review; no outcome tracking |
| Generic AI tools (ChatGPT, Copilot) | AI assistance | No decision documentation; no provenance; no institutional memory |
| Internal systems (SharePoint, Notion) | Document management | No structured decision lifecycle; no outcome tracking; no learning loop |

ChronoSentiment Enterprise is the only product that treats the investment decision as a structured lifecycle — from thesis formation through committee review to outcome recording and organisational learning.

## Go-to-Market

**Primary channel:** Direct sales (enterprise)
**Secondary channel:** Design partner programme (3–5 firms in Phase 1B)
**Pricing model:** Per-seat SaaS (annual), enterprise tier for larger firms
**Target price point:** £500–£2,000 per seat per year

**Entry motion:** Land with the Decision Workspace and Decision Timeline (fastest time-to-value for individual portfolio managers). Expand to committee review workflows and the Institutional Decision Knowledge Graph as the team adopts the product.

**Retention motion:** The Decision Archive creates switching costs that increase over time. The longer a firm uses ChronoSentiment Enterprise, the more valuable the product becomes.

---

## Roadmap Principles

1. **Decision lifecycle first** — every feature is evaluated by its contribution to the structured investment decision lifecycle.
2. **Provenance by default** — every piece of information in the system has a traceable source. No black boxes.
3. **Governance as infrastructure** — committee review workflows and audit trails are built in from the start, not retrofitted.
4. **Organisational learning as the moat** — features that make the Institutional Decision Knowledge Graph richer over time are more valuable than features that provide one-time utility.
5. **AI documentation as a first-class feature** — the AI's contribution to every decision is documented, not hidden.

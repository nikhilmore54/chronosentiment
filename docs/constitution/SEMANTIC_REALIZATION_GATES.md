# Semantic Realization Gates

## Purpose
This document defines the strict mechanical conditions under which semantic expansion—instantiating domain meaning, activating conceptual architecture, or expanding capabilities—is legitimately allowed. Its purpose is to prevent **legitimacy-by-momentum** and protect against expansion-by-success.

## Authorized Expansion Triggers
Semantic realization may reopen **ONLY** when all of the following conditions are met:
- **Operational Ambiguity Becomes Materially Costly**: The lack of a semantic layer is actively degrading the core mechanical stability or imposing unacceptable operational burdens.
- **Replay Boundaries Remain Stable Under Pressure**: The substrate has survived prolonged exposure to scarcity, isolation, and cross-platform verification without altering its causal outputs.
- **Authority Separation is Mechanically Preserved**: New capabilities can be added without leaking governance into the Observatory or orchestration layers.
- **Substrate Legitimacy is Operationally Proven**: The system has earned its trust through mechanical determinism, not just architectural elegance.

## Forbidden Triggers
Semantic realization must **NEVER** be reopened due to:
- **Expansion-by-Success**: Advancing capabilities simply because the current systems work perfectly.
- **Abstract Conceptual Completeness**: Fulfilling architectural diagrams or planned ontology structures before they are mechanically forced by reality.
- **Roadmap Momentum**: Pursuing feature completion goals decoupled from explicit operational pressure.

## Invariant
**Semantic expansion is an escalation triggered by necessity, not an assumption driven by momentum.**

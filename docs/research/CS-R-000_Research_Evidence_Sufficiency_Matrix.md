## Purpose

This document is the governance tool for the ChronoSentiment Research Series. It provides a single-page view of where the programme stands across all research questions, distinguishing between what secondary research has established and what primary research (Phase 1B) must validate.

It is designed to be updated after Phase 1B to show the transition from secondary to primary evidence, and to identify any remaining gaps that require further research before Phase 2 commercial investment.

## Status Definitions

| Status | Meaning |
|--------|---------|
| **Strong** | Multiple independent secondary sources; finding is well-established; primary research will refine but is unlikely to overturn |
| **Moderate** | Several secondary sources with some estimation; finding is directionally supported; primary research required to confirm |
| **Weak** | Limited secondary evidence; finding is plausible but not established; primary research is required before acting |
| **Unknown** | No secondary evidence; question can only be answered through primary research |

## Phase 1A → Phase 1B Transition Summary

**What Phase 1A (secondary research) has established:**

The market exists, is large, and is growing. The five core governance problems are real and evidenced across multiple independent sources. Regulatory convergence is increasing urgency. No incumbent appears to provide an integrated decision governance capability. The technology stack is feasible at MVP cost.

**What Phase 1B (primary research) must establish:**

Whether customers experience the problems as purchasing priorities. Who the economic buyer is and what the buying process looks like. Whether customers would pay for an integrated platform and at what price. Whether the "Financial Decision Governance" category framing resonates. Whether the problems are experienced as connected or as independent issues addressable by point solutions.

**The remaining uncertainty is not about whether the market or problems exist — it is about the commercial assumptions that only customer conversations can validate.**

## Phase 1B Interview Question Map

The following questions map directly from the sufficiency gaps above to Phase 1B interview topics:

| Sufficiency Gap | Phase 1B Interview Question |
|----------------|----------------------------|
| Purchasing urgency from regulation | "Which regulations are most relevant to your AI governance decisions? Have they influenced any technology purchases?" |
| Economic buyer identity | "Who would own the budget for a decision governance platform? CIO, compliance, technology?" |
| Buying process | "How do you typically evaluate and purchase new investment workflow tools? What does the decision process look like?" |
| Willingness to pay | "What would you expect to pay for a platform that solved [problem]? What budget category would this come from?" |
| Category resonance | "When I describe 'Financial Decision Governance' as a category, does that framing resonate with how you think about this problem?" |
| Integrated vs point solutions | "Do you see these governance problems as connected, or as separate issues you'd address with different tools?" |
| Execution validation gap | "How do you currently validate that trades were executed in line with the original investment intent?" |
| Institutional memory | "What happens to investment rationale and decision history when a portfolio manager leaves?" |
| AI governance urgency | "How are you currently tracking which AI tools influenced which investment decisions?" |

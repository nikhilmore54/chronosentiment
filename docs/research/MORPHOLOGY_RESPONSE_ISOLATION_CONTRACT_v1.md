# MORPHOLOGY RESPONSE ISOLATION CONTRACT v1

## 1. Constitutional Authority
This contract strictly governs the relationship between the laboratory's morphology instrumentation and its alpha/strategy cognition layer. As the laboratory gains the capability to observe multidimensional continuity deformation geometries, the risk of "optimization gravity" increases exponentially. This contract exists to construct an absolute firewall between *observing* how strategies respond to topologies and *adapting* strategies to survive topologies.

## 2. The Isolation Firewall
All strategy code, parameters, and cognition architectures must remain strictly **topology-blind**. The laboratory may observe how a strategy's internal geometry deforms under various topologies, but the strategy itself must never "know" about the morphology it is operating within.

The following practices are STRICTLY PROHIBITED:
- **Topology-Conditioned Strategy Comparison:** Ranking strategies (e.g., Strategy A is "better" than Strategy B) based on their morphological survivability.
- **Morphology-Conditioned Parameter Tuning:** Adjusting window depths, thresholds, or any other hyperparameter to "optimize" survival in specific convergence regimes (e.g., $NC_{\tau}$).
- **Adaptive Morphology Targeting:** Allowing strategies to attempt to classify the current topology and adapt their logic (e.g., "if in fast-wave deformation, shorten memory window").
- **Covariance-Driven Alpha Redesign:** Modifying alpha logic specifically to minimize occupancy density or transition entropy.

## 3. Permitted Observational Framing
The laboratory's relationship with strategies is strictly restricted to:
- **Strategy Morphology Response Observation:** Observing and describing how different cognition geometries deform, how memory structures behave, and how occupancy metrics evolve under bounded topological conditions.

Any output implying that a morphology is "desirable," "failed," "bad," or "optimal" for trading is a violation of this contract. We measure *bounded continuity deformation geometry*, not *desirable trading behavior*.

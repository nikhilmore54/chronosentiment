# ECONOMIC SEMANTICS CONTRACT v1

## 1. Constitutional Authority
This protocol governs the terminology and semantic framing of the Economic Ecology within the ChronoSentiment Execution Laboratory. As the laboratory transitions into measuring the economic outcomes of topological fracture, it is structurally imperative to prevent observational physics from being reframed as a predictive or optimization layer. 

## 2. Forbidden Terminology
The following terms imply desirability, superiority, or protective intelligence in environmental fragmentation. They are permanently banned from the architecture's variables, outputs, and documentation:

- ❌ "Ecology Damage" (implies canonical execution is uniformly superior)
- ❌ "Shielding" / "Physics Shield" (implies intentionality or protective quality)
- ❌ "Avoided Loss" / "Protected Loss" (implies foresight)
- ❌ "Beneficial Fragmentation" / "Good Fragmentation"
- ❌ "Economic Enhancement" / "Economic Improvement"

## 3. Approved Observational Terminology
The following terms enforce neutral measurement of realized vs canonical economic states and must be used exclusively:

- ✅ `Economic Divergence`: The raw numerical delta between Canonical PnL and Fragmented PnL.
- ✅ `Execution Non-Realization`: The mechanical state where canonical intent could not convert into economic exposure due to topological blockades.
- ✅ `Non-Executed Exposure`: The economic variance resulting from an unfulfilled intent sequence.
- ✅ `Positive Divergence Event`: A specific scenario where Fragmented PnL exceeds Canonical PnL due to non-realization of canonical risk.
- ✅ `Negative Divergence Event`: A specific scenario where Canonical PnL exceeds Fragmented PnL due to non-realization of canonical reward.

## 4. Fundamental Mission Statement
The Economic Ecology laboratory exists to map **economic survivability under topology deformation**, NOT to discover profitability. The fact that economic outcomes are topology-sensitive even when cognition is topology-blind is a physical observation, not a prescriptive trading rule. 

Any pull request or logic that attempts to convert expected Economic Divergence into an execution decision (e.g., dynamically altering position size or admissibility based on perceived "beneficial fragmentation") is a catastrophic violation of the causal architecture and must be rejected outright.

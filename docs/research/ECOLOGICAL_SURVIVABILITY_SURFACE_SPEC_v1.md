# ECOLOGICAL SURVIVABILITY SURFACE SPECIFICATION v1

## 1. Constitutional Authority
This specification governs the visualization and dimensional mapping of Execution-Qualified economic outcomes. The Ecological Survivability Surface is strictly a descriptive mapping of economic realization topology under environmental stress. It is explicitly NOT an optimization landscape or a profitability ranking mechanism. 

## 2. Forbidden Operations
The following operations are permanently prohibited when analyzing or mapping Survivability Surfaces:
1. **Scalar Compression**: Do not collapse multiple dimensions (e.g., PnL, coherence, TSI) into a single "Robustness Score," "Fitness Rating," or "Market IQ."
2. **Strategy Ranking**: Do not rank cognitive architectures on a 1D scale of "best to worst" survivability.
3. **Optimization Inference**: Do not use the surface to computationally derive dynamic execution rules or hyperparameters. 
4. **Desirability Framing**: Do not interpret positive economic divergence (where fragmented PnL > canonical PnL) as an optimized state.

## 3. Dimensional Mapping (The Four Planes)
The survivability surface must simultaneously project across four distinct observational planes to prevent causal collapse:

### 3.1. Topology Plane (Environmental Stress)
* **Topology Severity Index (TSI)**: The structural fragmentation magnitude.
* **Acceptance Ratio**: Visibility continuity and data admission rate.
* **Lag Dispersion (`lag_stddev`)**: Temporal propagation fragmentation.
* **Recovery Half-Life**: Memory persistence and chronological scar duration.

### 3.2. State Plane (Cognitive Integrity)
* **MCI State Overlap Ratio**: Memory Coherence Index retention.
* **Cognitive Divergences**: Count of structural intent fractures.
* **Replay Stability**: Certification of determinism under stress.

### 3.3. Economic Plane (Realized Physics)
* **Canonical PnL**: Theoretical outcome under perfect visibility.
* **Fragmented PnL**: Realized outcome under execution constraints.
* **Economic Divergence**: Mechanical variance (`Canonical - Fragmented`).
* **Execution Non-Realization Rate**: Percentage of intent sequences physically blocked by the environment.

### 3.4. Chronology Plane (Causal Truth)
* **Ecology Fingerprint Continuity**: Guarantee of unbroken substrate observation.
* **Replay Hash Integrity**: Mathematical proof of sequence immutability.
* **State Reconstruction Boundary**: Quarantine validation for Synthetic Repair layers.

## 4. Fundamental Interpretation Rule
The surface must only answer:
> *"What physically happens to execution-qualified economics when chronology visibility deforms?"*

The system must never be asked:
> *"Which strategy should we deploy?"*

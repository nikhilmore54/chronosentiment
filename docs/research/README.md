# ChronoSentiment Research Lineage & Historical Context

This directory preserves the historical documents, conceptual research, and architectural exploration that led to the creation of ChronoSentiment. 

These documents describe a larger, more ambitious product universe: a deterministic execution simulation, cognition platform, and execution realism environment. They establish the philosophical framing and theoretical topologies that drove the initial engineering effort.

> [!WARNING]
> **These documents are preserved intentionally as research lineage and future product exploration, but they are not part of the active operational scope governed by the `GOVERNANCE.md` freeze policy.**

## Why Preserve Them?

ChronoSentiment Core is the strictly bounded, operational replay and divergence infrastructure. 
However, the research lineage contained here provides immense value for:
* Execution realism framing.
* Deterministic market simulation concepts.
* Event-driven market reconstruction topologies.
* Execution-aware explainability.

Treat these files as candidate future product application layers built upon the hardened deterministic infrastructure, rather than descriptions of the current operational tool.

# REPAIR SEMANTICS CONTRACT v1

## 1. Constitutional Authority
This protocol governs the terminology and semantic framing of the Synthetic Repair Ecology within the ChronoSentiment Execution Laboratory. It explicitly outlaws the use of evaluative, optimization-focused language when describing adaptive continuity reconstruction. The laboratory's function is strictly limited to measuring how cognition behaves under incomplete chronology, NOT forcing cognition to align with historical truth.

## 2. Forbidden Terminology
The following terms imply optimization objectives, value judgments, or narrative benchmarks. They are permanently banned from the architecture's variables, outputs, and documentation:

- ❌ "successful repairs"
- ❌ "false hallucinations"
- ❌ "repair success rate"
- ❌ "improved cognition"
- ❌ "better continuity"
- ❌ "catastrophic continuity"
- ❌ "repair quality"
- ❌ "repair intelligence"

## 3. Approved Observational Terminology
The following terms enforce neutral measurement of topological physics and must be used exclusively:

- ✅ `canonical_intent_matches`: The count of instances where the synthetically repaired intent aligned with the canonical intent, while the raw fragmented intent diverged.
- ✅ `repair_induced_divergences`: The count of instances where the raw fragmented intent matched the canonical intent, but the repair interpolation caused a new divergence.
- ✅ `high divergence amplification`: A state where repair-induced divergences significantly outnumber canonical intent matches.
- ✅ `topology reconstruction stability`: The mechanical capacity of a repair algorithm to maintain state equivalence without amplifying divergence.
- ✅ `canonical alignment rate`: The statistical percentage of mechanical alignment between the synthetic layer and canonical history.

## 4. Fundamental Mission Statement
The repair laboratory exists to measure **whether fractured memory is repairable without violating causal truth**. It does not exist to "fix" trading signals. Missing chronology is recognized as a valid environmental constraint, and the raw fragmented universe is explicitly acknowledged as occasionally being MORE causally faithful than a synthetically repaired universe.

Any pull request introducing logic that explicitly optimizes for `canonical_intent_matches` at the expense of topology isolation is a violation of the causal architecture and must be rejected.

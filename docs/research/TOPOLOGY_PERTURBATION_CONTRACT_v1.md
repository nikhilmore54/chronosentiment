# TOPOLOGY PERTURBATION CONTRACT v1

## 1. Core Mandate
The Synthetic Fragmentation Injector is exclusively an experimental deformation tool for visibility topology. Its sole purpose is to mutate the temporal visibility metrics (lag, dispersion) in order to stress-test the Execution Physics intersection. It is **not** a market simulator.

## 2. Prohibited Semantic Mutations
- **Chronology Modification**: The injector MUST NEVER alter the deterministic sequencing of the `barrier_ts`, nor may it manipulate the values, ordering, or ingestion of the underlying price candles.
- **Signal Mutability**: The injector MUST NEVER influence strategy intent logic. The Alpha engine must remain perfectly ignorant of the injected topology.
- **Admissibility Policy Alternation**: The injector MUST NEVER override the Execution Governor's strict boolean intersection logic. It may only alter the upstream inputs (the `freshness` telemetry).

## 3. Allowable Perturbation Domains
The injector operates strictly between **Acquisition** and **Observability**. It is allowed only to manipulate the lag timestamps of incoming packets (e.g., synthetically adding a 180s delay to 50% of the cohort). It produces a deformed `live_session_steps.jsonl` ledger that mechanically forces the Admissibility Governor into new edge states (e.g., `DEGRADED_OBSERVABILITY`).

## 4. Replay Invariants
During any synthetic fragmentation event:
- The cryptographic **Replay Artifact Hash** generated by the execution harness MUST change to reflect the deformed admissibility constraints.
- The **Alpha Intent Sequence** MUST remain perfectly identical to the baseline un-perturbed run, certifying causal isolation.

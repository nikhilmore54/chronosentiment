# Execution Directive — Transition from Architecture to Product Delivery

**Date:** 2026-07-22

## Architecture Program Status

The Coralys Architecture Program is **formally closed**.

The constitutional architecture has been validated through implementation, benchmark preservation, repository convergence, and governance alignment.

The repository, documentation, and dependency structure are now consistent.

No further architectural work shall be undertaken unless triggered by implementation evidence, benchmark evidence, pilot evidence, or repeated product evidence.

Architecture is no longer an independent workstream.

---

# Execution Begins

Effective immediately, the project transitions from **Architecture** to **Execution**.

The governing objective is now:

> **P-001 — UltraCrew Minimum Sellable Product**

Engineering effort shall be directed toward delivering a deployable, demonstrable, customer-ready workforce scheduling product.

Success is no longer measured by architectural refinement.

Success is measured by:

* usable software,
* successful demonstrations,
* pilot deployments,
* measurable customer outcomes,
* and commercial validation.

---

# Execution Priorities

## Priority 1 — UltraCrew (Commercial Flagship)

Primary engineering effort: **80–90%**

Objectives:

* Complete P-001
* Complete remaining application functionality
* Remove remaining architectural boundary violations
* Prepare demonstration environment
* Prepare pilot deployment
* Support WOA and WDX engagements
* Achieve first measurable customer improvement

---

## Priority 2 — ChronoSentiment (Internal Laboratory)

Secondary engineering effort: **10–20%**

Objectives:

* Daily personal use
* Continuous refinement
* Validate decision workflows
* Stress-test Coralys capabilities
* Record implementation observations
* Improve usability through real usage

ChronoSentiment is not currently a commercial objective. It is an evidence-generation system.

---

# Platform Governance

The Coralys platform evolves only when justified by evidence.

Permitted drivers are:

* implementation evidence,
* benchmark evidence,
* pilot evidence,
* repeated product evidence.

Speculative platform evolution is prohibited.

---

# Engineering Principle

From this point onward:

> Products drive platform evolution.

Not:

> Platform drives products.

Every platform enhancement must have a demonstrated need from multiple products.

# Immediate Engineering Focus

The engineering backlog shall now be evaluated exclusively against one question:

> **Does this help deliver P-001?**

If the answer is **yes**, it belongs on the critical path.

If **no**, it is deferred unless it generates implementation evidence through the ChronoSentiment laboratory or is required to maintain the constitutional architecture.

---

# Mission

The objective of the next phase is straightforward:

**Deliver UltraCrew as the first commercially validated Coralys product while using ChronoSentiment to continuously validate and strengthen the platform through real-world use.**

**Execution begins now.**

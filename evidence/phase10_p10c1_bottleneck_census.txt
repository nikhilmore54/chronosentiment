=== C1-A Observations ===

1. The bottleneck structure is highly concentrated. In each large instance, one arc
   accounts for >74% of all capacity violation events. This is not diffuse overload.

2. The secondary arcs (659, 303, 969) are structurally adjacent to the dominant arcs
   in their respective instances. This suggests the dominant arc and its secondary may
   share a common network segment or demand set.

3. Saturation levels are moderate (mean 1.09–1.23, max 1.26–1.39). These are not
   extreme overloads — they are in the range where rerouting might plausibly help,
   if alternative paths exist.

4. setA-16 has the highest saturation (max_sat=1.3893 on arc 606) and the most
   complex secondary structure (606/303/302/414). This instance may have a more
   constrained topology than setA-13 or setA-19.

5. The [diag] coverage gap (setA-04/14 = 0, setA-06/10 = low) means C1-A cannot
   characterize bottleneck arcs for those instances. This is a data limitation of
   the existing P10-C0 instrumentation, not a new finding.

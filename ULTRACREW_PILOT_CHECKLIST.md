# UltraCrew Pilot Readiness Checklist

> **Status**: Pilot Methodology v1.0
> **Date**: 2026-07-19
> **Purpose**: Confirm that both the customer organisation and the UltraCrew team are prepared before a pilot begins. This checklist is completed jointly during or immediately after the Discovery workshop.
> **Evolves when**: Pilot delivery experience shows the process should change — not when architecture changes or customer conversations suggest different messaging.

---

## How to Use This Checklist

Complete this checklist with the customer during the Discovery workshop. Every item should have a clear owner and a confirmed status before the pilot start date is agreed.

Items marked **Mandatory** must be confirmed before the pilot begins. Items marked **Recommended** reduce delivery risk but do not block the pilot.

A pilot should not start until all Mandatory items are confirmed.
